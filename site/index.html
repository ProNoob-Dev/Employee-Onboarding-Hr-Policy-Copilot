Questions about the copilot.

Is the copilot real client work?

No. It is an invented demo with synthetic policy documents, questions and employee ids. There is no real client and no real HR data.

Does the copilot send anything to HR?

No. The routing agent writes a draft reply and stores it on the case. An HR reviewer approves or rejects it through the API; nothing is emailed or messaged.

What happens when the copilot does not know?

When the best match scores below 0.10, the question is routed to HR as low-confidence, with the same draft-and-review path as a sensitive case.

Does the copilot need Azure or Postgres?

No. It runs fully offline with a local answerer and in-memory TF-IDF. Azure OpenAI and pgvector switch on only when their environment variables are set.
